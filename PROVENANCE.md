## General inspiration

- [addyosmani/agent-skills](https://github.com/addyosmani/agent-skills): deliberate
  problem definition and engineering workflows.
- [mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills): conversations that
  clarify intent and retrospectives that improve the next run.
- [obra/superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers): incremental development,
  behavior-focused tests, and review as part of the workflow.

The automatic workflow's implementation-quality reference also draws on general
craft principles from the author's repository-specific backend and frontend
guidance. It is rewritten without company-specific code, commands, or paths.

Upstream skill files are not bundled as authored work. When incorporating an
upstream adaptation, record its source and retain applicable attribution and
license notices.
